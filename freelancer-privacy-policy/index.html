Freelancer Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Freelancer

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Freelancer is a budget, income and debt-payoff tracker for freelancers, published by Consumer Concepts, LLC (Omni Consumer Concepts, "we," "us").

Short version: everything you enter stays on your phone. We don't collect it, we never see it, and there's no account to create.

What the app stores

The app saves the information you type into it on your own device, using your phone's local app storage. That includes:

  • Your income entries, split by client or source
  • Your monthly budget categories and amounts
  • Your debts, balances, interest rates and payments
  • Your mileage entries
  • Your app settings

Where it goes

Nowhere. There are no servers behind this app, no user accounts and no login. Your information is not uploaded, synced, shared or sold, because it never leaves your device in the first place.

We do not collect personal information. We do not use analytics, advertising or third-party tracking tools in the app.

Backup and restore

The app includes an optional backup feature that you start yourself. When you tap Backup, the app creates a file of your data and hands it to your phone's own share options, so you can save it wherever you choose, such as your files, your email or a cloud drive you control.

That file and where it ends up are entirely in your hands. We don't receive it and we have no access to it. Restoring simply reads a backup file you pick.

Purchases

Freelancer offers in-app purchases. Purchases are handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or receive your payment details. Those stores' own privacy policies apply to the purchase.

Review prompt

The app may show a message asking if you'd like to leave a review. If you tap through, it opens the App Store or Google Play listing. You can ignore it, and nothing about you is sent to us either way.

Your control over your data

  • You can edit or delete any entry inside the app at any time.
  • Deleting the app from your device deletes the data stored with it. If you want to keep a copy first, use the backup feature before deleting.
  • Because we never receive your data, there's nothing for us to delete on our end and no request you need to send us.

Children

Freelancer is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children. The app collects no personal information from anyone.

Features we may add later

We're considering an optional feature that would let you connect online store accounts (for example eBay, Etsy or Shopify) so your sales income is tracked automatically.

This feature does not exist today, and nothing described here is happening now. If we build it, here is how it would work:

  • It would be optional. The app would keep working exactly as it does now if you never connect a store.
  • You would connect a store through that store's own sign-in screen. We would never ask for or see your store password.
  • Access would be read-only, limited to what's needed to total your sales.
  • We would receive sales amounts and dates, not your customers' personal or payment details.
  • You would be able to disconnect a store at any time, from inside the app and from your store account's own settings.

Before any version with this feature is released, we will update this policy with a new effective date, update our Apple and Google data-safety disclosures, and tell you inside the app. Until you see that, the app collects nothing.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, we'll update this page and change the effective date at the top. Continuing to use the app after an update means the updated policy applies.
